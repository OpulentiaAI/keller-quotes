## Goals and evidence gaps

1. Establish more than 90% independently reviewed, completed quoting under unchanged criteria. This goal has **not** been met by historical replay, synthetic tests or correct holds.
2. Connect the initial client request and first quote to revisions, the issued quote, order lines, invoices, cash allocations and closed-job costs. Use [[map - Keller quote lifecycle gap closure]] and [[quote-lifecycle-gap-closure]] to separate estimate error from changed scope and unverified outcomes.
3. Improve one corroborated failure mechanism at a time, preserve every attempted case and regression, and confirm changes on untouched evidence before activation.
