# Source, quote, and internal order contracts

The workflow separates a reconciled recorded customer price, an estimator prediction against historical targets, and a complete **internal** proposal. None alone authenticates issuance, current manufacturing cost, customer acceptance or commercial readiness. Use the [quote-estimator skill](../.agents/skills/keller-quote-estimator/SKILL.md) for the canonical procedure: complete intake, draft once, inspect contributors, one deduplicated exact evidence phase with bounded continuations, supported pricing assumptions/ranges, gross margin, independent challenge and named human review. The [Steve operator companion](steve-pricing-workflow.md) maps retained sources to fields and distinguishes measurement claims. Keep customer evidence, requests and reports private outside the checkout; examples are synthetic.

## Source choice and read-only evidence

`quotes.csv` is a frozen 179,608-break/40,111-part-quote register of **internal calculations** with 2,210 placeholders, not issued customer prices. Its posted-history `won`/`open` labels do not establish wins, losses, payment or revenue. The separately validated document register contains `customer_quote_pdf` prices with **unknown** outcome. Select an immutable Polygres corpus ID explicitly, following [the connection/TLS and corpus guide](../.agents/skills/polygres/SKILL.md). No global latest pointer exists. Ingestion time is not quote time. Raw CSV and typed price columns, not an `original_row` JSONB, hold the source rows. Historical/mixed complete-order requests require explicit `--register` or native corpus. Fully supplied `unit_price`, `cost_plus` and `should_cost` requests omit it and require no database/export. Only the older estimate CLI defaults to internal calculations. Retrieval and exports are read-only, not approval to import another corpus.

Customer-PDF text search locates pages but is not a price oracle. Reconciled quote-letter rows supply recorded customer-price evidence: verify quantity, full source unit price (up to five decimals), printed extension, letter/date, source field, PDF and transcript hashes. Printed unit text may differ from the unrounded price; explain only verified differences. Keep each curve on one compatible provenanced source. A supplier PO, invoice or packing slip is not a customer-quotation break; an invoice does not prove payment and packing does not prove invoicing. The derivative `quote_date` records reconciled inquiry/header metadata, not necessarily the original `QUOTEN` date or authenticated issuance date; `date_stamp` includes known revision/letter information. Reconcile the price-bearing PDF's printed By/footer date before claiming prior availability.

As of the independently verified 2026-09-28 import, the **whole corpus** covered 39,975 PDF records/75,096 pages; 42,873 verified customer-quote breaks came from a **subset of 7,845 price-source PDFs**. Poppler independently restored searchable text for 164 original AnyDoc failures, while original status and hashes remained intact. The eight original core tables were full-row-hash identical and vector counts unchanged. Database size was 500,291,251 bytes (~477.1 MiB), leaving ~22.9 MiB nominal under the documented 500 MiB cap. These are dated observations: actual size/allocation must be measured before any write, and a repeated full corpus needs a separate capacity decision. No document graph or embedding registration occurred. See [additive evidence design and import guards](polygres-document-evidence.md); `scripts/load.py` is **not** the document importer.

## Source basis and quote-time boundaries

Internal `QUOTQTYS` calculations and recorded customer-PDF prices are different bases; never substitute one for the other or present either as current manufacturing cost. For historical replay, exclude the target source `quote_no` across all breaks and restrict other quote, letter, revision and known outcome events to strictly before the requested date. A verified letter date must come from the price-bearing source, not an assumed ingestion or derivative metadata date. Missing source identity or conflicting chronology cannot establish eligibility. Preserve that prior-availability blocker; it is not a blanket ban on prospective use of reviewed historical assumptions. Frozen rows may contain unversioned later edits, so metadata-cutoff snapshot evidence is not a true backtest. Missing prices are not zero; diagnostic bands and unverified outcomes never authorize unattended pricing.

The estimator screens at most the top `min(screenTopN, rankLimit, 12)` ranked analogs per line (defaults: 3 and 8). Explicit `admit` verdicts normally reach strategy selection and pricing. When enabled Jev admits none, the highest-ranked screened usable candidate may instead support a clearly labeled review-only provisional fallback; its rejected/quarantined verdict is preserved through strategy selection and the output, never relabeled as admission. Explicit engineering conflicts, unavailable screening and unscreened tails are ineligible. `screenTopN: 0` holds pricing, and offline screening has no provisional fallback. Returned analogs include every candidate passed to pricing, without a five-reference display cap. Each reference also carries source quote date, letter date, revision, last-touch date and price evidence for review. The screening limit is a resource budget, not permission to trust an unscreened fallback. Historical `median_won` is a strategy name, not proof of a confirmed sale: customer-PDF price rows have unknown outcomes, while legacy posted-history labels are unverified.

## Complete internal order, not customer delivery

The order CLI is offline, pins the chosen register hash, and creates `order.json` plus `order.md` in a **new** directory. Its request needs order ID, date, customer, unique stable `line_id` per part, part number or description, positive integer piece quantity, and explicit shipping and tax **amounts**. Preserve supplied `customer_id` and distinct part/drawing identities; document scope and unresolved price-critical questions. An omitted `pricing` invokes historical analog retrieval before `quote_date`; no usable amount leaves the line unpriced. An operator `unit_price` is positive, at most four decimals, and needs a reason. `cost_plus` requires nonnegative `material_per_unit`, `labor_per_unit`, `outside_per_unit`, `setup_total`, `margin_pct` and reason. Every zero needs a real basis, not a missing input disguised as zero. Supported engineering estimates/ranges and approved assumptions can support a prospective proposal without accepted-order/payment/actual-job ledgers; they do not guarantee actual cost.

Each line reports `proposal_status` (`NUMERIC_PROVISIONAL` or `MISSING`), `evidence_status`, `next_action` and `uncertainties` alongside unit/extension fields; use the exact enums and contributor fields in [`types.ts`](../estimator/src/types.ts) and [`order.ts`](../estimator/src/order.ts). Existing labels such as `VERIFIED_CUSTOMER_PDF` describe price provenance, not authenticated issuance or engineering equivalence. A finite backend amount stays visible even if adoption is declined. Preserve the unchanged draft and `pricing_decisions[].proposed_unit_price` when required, with separate adoption/readiness reasons in supported handoff fields. Explicit incompatibility blocks transfer, unknown current cost blocks supported margin claims but not historical comparison, and unverified actual ledgers do not block supported prospective estimating. `MISSING` means no usable amount, never license for a fallback. Complete arithmetic is not a completed adopted or commercially ready quote.

After inspecting contributors, resolve named price-critical gaps through one deduplicated retained-evidence phase, including bounded page/memo continuations. Ask one consolidated follow-up only for remaining critical inputs after available relevant evidence is exhausted or its access/budget limit is recorded. Prefer current valid supplier offers, then approved contemporaneous purchasing evidence/current confirmed stock-cost basis, then explicitly labeled estimates and sensitivity. Record effective/expiry dates and COST-versus-SELL meaning; steel benchmarks bound trends, not delivered buy offers. A stale source cannot be presented as a live price.

### Optional cost worksheet

`pricing.cost_basis` is required on `should_cost` and optional on `cost_plus`; `should_cost` derives flat amounts through the existing cost-plus margin arithmetic, without inventing any input. An ALL-lines fully supplied native request may omit corpus; corpus/register provenance is then null, source rows zero. Explicitly selected corpora still require export, and scoped evaluation transport still requires its corpus. The exact JSON contract/validation is defined by [`OrderPricing`](../estimator/src/order.ts) and [`CostBasis`](../estimator/src/costing.ts), not a second schema here. Existing explicit-price and flat cost-plus paths remain available. The worksheet records source/hash/locator, original units/conversion, commercial dates, quantity/minimum/yield, low/base/high estimates, rate meaning, charge inclusion and assumptions for reconciliation. The optional line `cost_breakdown` reports reconciliation and estimated line cost/margin ranges; its assertions are supplied, not independently authenticated. Respect its production-line scope; reconcile excluded shipping/tax/order charges separately. Unknown components are not zero, and a worksheet cannot override explicit conflict or certify freshness merely by declaring it.

### Optional retained intake

Exact contracts are in [`intake.ts`](../estimator/src/intake.ts): order `intake` binds original request bytes and attachments to SHA-256/locators; line `source_evidence` carries attachment, page/record/field citations; `geometry` carries source-attributed facts with original units, applicability, uncertainty and review; `uom` preserves reviewed original-unit-to-piece conversion. `geometry` is not verified CAD. Worksheet entries may reference reviewed supported/assumed `engineering_fact_ids`; conflicting/unknown facts cannot justify cost entries. Extracted facts never automatically generate costs. Every explicit engineering conflict blocks completion/adoption (`BLOCKED`, null total) on all pricing paths, including unreferenced facts; numeric comparisons remain visible. Unknown/assumed facts retain uncertainty and named review.

Explicit operator retention: `node estimator/node_modules/tsx/dist/cli.mjs estimator/src/intake-cli.ts "$PRIVATE_ORDER_JSON" --attachments "$PRIVATE_UPLOAD_MANIFEST_JSON" --operator "$NAME"`. The manifest is an array of `{id,path,media_type}` for up to 20 local files (32 MiB each, 64 MiB total). Use `attachment_id` source evidence and `upload:<id>` cost-source locators in original input; retention fills hashes/locators. Original JSON (max 128 KiB), attachments and enriched request are retained in a new owner-only UUID directory under `~/.local/share/keller-quotes/intake`; immutable-by-interface files are mode 0400 and completed directory 0500. Submission verifies retained hashes and that the structured request still derives from the retained original; changed inputs require a new capture. This is not cryptographic authentication of operator review or automatic email/CAD ingestion. [Synthetic example](../estimator/examples/should-cost-intake.json) demonstrates supported shape, not production prices. Human review remains mandatory, and native quote review binds the enriched request hash that includes the original-input/attachment hashes.

The manual distinguishes estimated setup/run COST at operation-maintenance cost rates from quote-specific SELL rates (see [source citation and formulas](steve-pricing-workflow.md#cost-sell-and-estimating-time)). Compute setup occurrences × minutes / 60 × COST rate, plus process quantity × run minutes per piece / 60 × COST rate; allocate shared/repeated setups and included overhead only once. SELL rates cannot silently become cost-plus costs. Approved time estimates are permissible planning inputs, not verified actual minutes. If the deployed API does not accept the worksheet, retain it in private review notes without adding unsupported keys or claiming machine validation.

Cost-plus sell unit = `(material + labor + outside + setup / quantity) / (1 - margin_pct / 100)`. `margin_pct` is **gross margin**, not markup, and lies from zero inclusive to 100 exclusive. Unit pricing rounds half-up to four decimals; displayed extension rounds half-up to cents. Retain the separate up-to-five-decimal source-PDF evidence rather than overwriting its precision. A positive unit price whose extension rounds to zero cents remains a visible `NUMERIC_PROVISIONAL` amount but blocks order completion (`BLOCKED`, null total) on every pricing path, even with other priced lines or charges. Resolve the zero-cent extension through reviewed pricing before completion; no price is raised automatically and there is no implicit free-line authorization. Line subtotals remain numeric diagnostics when every line has a number. This guard is not a general margin floor: positive-cent extensions can still miss a margin target. Shipping and tax are dollar amounts; zero is valid only when explicitly supplied, while absent/null blocks completion. Optional `additional_charges` have a label and nonnegative cent amount. There is no automatic tax/shipping engine.

```sh
node estimator/node_modules/tsx/dist/cli.mjs estimator/src/order-cli.ts \
  estimator/examples/order-request.json --register "$VERIFIED_CSV" \
  --out "$NEW_PRIVATE_ORDER_DIRECTORY"
```

The example is wholly synthetic; the chosen register is explicit even though both synthetic lines use operator pricing. Exit `0` / `PRICED_REQUIRES_REVIEW` means all lines and charges reconcile as an **internal proposal**, not approval. Exit `3` / `BLOCKED` still writes artifacts with `total: null`; `subtotal` is null if **any line** is missing, but can still show a complete line subtotal when only shipping or tax is missing. `priced_subtotal` is diagnostic only. Exit `2` indicates validation/runtime error. The JSON request hash covers `JSON.stringify(request)`, not file whitespace; artifacts retain register bytes hash (or null when no register is used), cutoff/offline mode, operator costs, analogs and warnings. **Do not send these internal artifacts or another customer's historical analog/cost details to a customer.** A named human must review the scope, costs, assumptions, quote price and redacted customer-safe output before an existing approved delivery process acts. No automatic booking, email, payment, fulfillment or scheduler enablement follows from the CLI. The local inbox worker routes complete order-shaped requests through `order-cli.ts` and retains this completeness state, including `BLOCKED` and `total: null`. Legacy estimate-shaped inbox requests still emit the older line-estimate draft schema and do not enforce the complete-order contract.
