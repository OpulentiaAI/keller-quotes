## What the current evidence establishes

An independent reconstruction of every prediction in the frozen, cutoff-aware 50-case verified customer-price replay found **8/50 all-pass (16%)**: 48 priced, 40 priced misses, and two holds; median absolute percentage error (APE) was 63.37% on priced cases. Exposed source-exclusion and cutoff checks passed in all 50 under the **old exposed-reference checks**, not a complete audit of contributors. Pricing used 372 actual analog contributors but exposed only 234 references; 230 contributors were unscreened and seven top-three candidates were quarantined. Forty-eight prices used fallback medians, none exact-part analogs; none of the 50 cases had another quote for that exact part even before cutoff filtering. Of 37 high-confidence cases, only eight passed. The request consumed only `part_no`, `description`, quantity, and `customer_id`; this does not establish manufacturing equivalence.

On the same 50 cases, the screened-admitted guard replay priced 47, held three, passed **9/50 (18%)**, and had 64.33% priced-case median APE. Versus 48 priced/two holds/8 pass/63.37% originally, this is a safety-boundary change with mixed pricing results, **not** a general accuracy gain or a 90% result. Both are offline historical replays, not complete live-agent tests; a hold is not a completed accurate quote.

The manufacturing-source audit found historical material values and operation rates/variables, but no positive active `SUTIME`/`RUNTIME` and no vendor quote valid on 2026-09-27. It does **not** say all cost data is absent. Historical customer prices are neither actual costs nor optimal prices; issued-price documents also do not establish wins, payment, or profit.

The verified read-only TLS Polygres corpus access establishes a data source, **not** an agent decision workflow: the [current local worker](local-automation.md) reads offline CSV, not Polygres. ## Pre-register and adjudicate the challenge

Before running a candidate, freeze quote-case eligibility, exclusions, grouped part/customer leakage boundaries, time-disjoint cutoff, source snapshots, workflow version, scoring rubric, and hold policy. Use a blind challenge set with targets and later outcomes withheld from the workflow; record every attempted eligible quote case in the denominator, including failures, invalid outputs, timeouts, and holds. A case exclusion or separately safety-adjudicated blocked-case cohort must be designated before seeing its result, never moved post hoc to improve the quote score. Report cohort composition and priced/held/blocked/error counts separately so a refusal-only policy cannot pass as a completed quoting service.

For each attempt, retain private, independently reviewable request, tools and authorization traces, source citations and immutable as-of cutoffs, typed-organization access, reasoning inputs, calculations, output, and human disposition. An actual integration run must demonstrate verified read-only Polygres reads **and** correct Ars Umbris typed-organization/runtime calls and scope; mocked traces and offline CSV replay cannot count as live access. Reviewers independently adjudicate source fitness, quantities and units of measure, routing/process assumptions, material and outside-process cost basis, current vendor-price freshness, arithmetic, uncertainty, safe holds, and whether a usable priced draft was completed. They must not grade the model against its own explanations, treat historical prices as optimum targets, or let an LLM judge itself. Missing or stale evidence must be cited as a blocker, not silently filled in.

## Separate gates; no substitutions

1. **Regression and safety:** Preserve the existing historical ±20% price criterion and its all-attempted-case accounting; compare against the same frozen inputs, and disclose priced-only error, completion, holds, and source/cutoff violations separately. Do not weaken tolerance, relabel holds as accurate, or accept cleaner citations as a price-quality pass. Any unsafe release or source leakage fails its safety check; report correctly blocked cases separately.
2. **Completed-quote decision quality:** Target **at least 90% completed, independently supported quoting all-pass over all attempted eligible quote cases** in the live workflow; every required criterion must pass per quote. Holds remain in that denominator and cannot count as completed-quote passes. Independently review justified holds on pre-adjudicated blocked cases as a separate safety/decision result, never a substitute for this 90% goal. Record reviewer disagreements and resolution against independent job evidence. Do not claim 90% without independent job labels and live workflow traces, or redefine the unchanged historical ±20% diagnostic and its current 9/50 all-pass. All required code/review checks must pass with no unresolved review findings. A synthetic 11/11 workflow test, passing unit tests, or an offline replay cannot satisfy this gate.
3. **Business outperformance:** Claim “better decisions than Keller” only after a paired, independently assessed comparison on **identical jobs** against a documented Keller human baseline, with actual cost, profit/margin, and customer/order outcome evidence (and timing and coverage) on both sides. Predefine the comparison metric and decision rule before observing results. Without those observations, report price agreement and operational readiness only—not superiority or a measured Keller error rate.

The 90% goal is a future acceptance target, not a current result or authority to send customer quotes autonomously. A passing draft still requires the approved human release gate.

## Executable source adjudication and decision scoring

Use the [source-bound assessment workflow](ground-truth-assessment.md) to turn source/rubric questions into a private versioned all-case assessment and bounded remediation queue. `python -B scripts/keller-ground-truth.py assess` independently opens the explicitly supplied original DBF/PDF/transcript bytes and keeps the six evidence domains separate; it never modifies the historical target or grade. Unsupported acceptance/current-cost/optimum claims remain unsupported, and operator assertions never become independent truth.

`python -B scripts/keller-ground-truth.py decisions` invokes the separate completed-quote evaluator, not the historical metric implementation. It consumes frozen full-RFQ eligibility/requirements, an independently source-bound assessment, the sealed actual attempt ledger/artifacts, named independent review execution and human handoff. Every criterion must pass for quote completion. Missing/dropped/invalid/held/error/timeout attempts remain intact, while justified predeclared holds get only a separate safety result. Signed/magnitude proposal errors and variance retain their explicit historical basis. Its observed rate does not certify live authenticity, independent job labels or blind isolation, so synthetic/exposed diagnostics cannot establish the 90% gate and the existing ±20% regression stays unchanged.
