# Repaired-context development iteration

This iteration did not recover complete quoting performance: **0/6 attempts completed all ten criteria, and no final price was adopted**. The six fresh Luna-max workers ran after the worker-context separation at commit `378738aa`; their answers and complete scoped MCP audits were sealed before two fresh, independent Luna-max judges evaluated them. All original criterion texts, weights and thresholds were preserved through the normative-only rubric projection.

These are development diagnostics, not independent confirmation. Three cases repeat earlier development requests and three additional cases already had an aborted attempt. Earlier contexts exposed prior aggregate outcomes; code, scopes and guidance also changed. Native-session snapshots are partial, and shared-account restrictions are instructional rather than OS-enforced. A separate chronology defect was also observed in the delivered evidence. None of those limitations removes a failed or held attempt from the denominator.

## Numerical errors and trajectories

The audits contain eight successful numeric tool proposals across six cases. Both second proposals repeated their respective first prices exactly. Counting those repetitions as new independent successes would distort coverage.

| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| First tool proposal, one per case | 6 | 60.28% | −29.10% | 3,844.21 pp² | 1/6 |
| Second tool proposal, only the two repeated cases | 2 | 51.33% | −51.33% | 1,395.50 pp² | 1/2 |
| Adopted final prices | 0 of 6 attempts | Undefined | Undefined | Undefined | 0 completed priced attempts |

The first and second rows are different cohorts: the two paired changes in absolute percentage error were both exactly zero. Variance uses the numeric-observation population denominator `n`, not `n−1`; it is the variance of signed percentage errors, measured in percentage points squared. Held prices are never inserted as numeric zeros. These proposal errors remain retrospective diagnostics, including the chronology-conflicted attempt, not validated prospective accuracy.

For the three repeated requests alone, the historical first-automatic proposals had MAPE **90.27%** and signed-error variance **7,385.58 pp²**, versus **91.61%** and **7,541.36 pp²** now. Two prices were unchanged; B1 Q1 worsened by **4.02 percentage points of absolute error**. That changed case also changed its structured material/finish-versus-notes representation and its selected analog identities, so this is not an isolated test of the quantity-weighting code. Both three-case cohorts remained 0/3 within ±20%.

All six frozen scopes had zero eligible exact-normalized-part rows. Every first tool draft used the offline median fallback over three analogs. The workers inspected source applicability rather than accepting those amounts, and did not establish the required geometry/material/process or quantity equivalence. One case was outside every cited analog quantity range. This identifies an evidence-admission limitation in these observed workflows; it does not prove that every other document in the corpus lacks a usable analogy.

## Integrity and verification corrections

Inspection of all 30 delivered page responses found printed By/footer dates on each. Four were later than the corresponding register dates; one was on or after the RFQ cutoff. That page described an updated quote, while its price metadata used the earlier Inquiry Date. The old builder accepted its numeric rows without checking the footer. A later printed version does not prove original issuance, but it defeats the claim that inquiry/header agreement alone establishes prior availability.

The subsequent read-only audit covered the exact union of **7,733 eligible documents and 7,744 pages** across the six scopes. It found **411 documents with later printed dates than their metadata**, **7,322 with internally consistent date fields**, and no missing or unparseable labelled dates under the existing MM/DD convention. **38 unique documents** have printed dates on or after at least one eligible cutoff, representing **47 scope-document comparisons across five scopes**. Scope memberships overlap; these are not 47 unique documents. Parent verification independently rehashed every page and reproduced these counts.

The patched parser held **all 411 conflicting documents** and accepted the other 7,322 at the document-parsing stage. This validates the targeted date-conflict guard, not original issuance, full source-price reconciliation or business accuracy. Even matching dates do not prove historical availability, and date-format ambiguity remains recorded in the private audit.

The source-date investigation and verifier fix are separate from the sealed grading. Existing corpora and scopes are not repaired by changing the builder; the broader scope findings remain an integrity addendum, not rewritten scores. The complete audit is an operator artifact, never a worker or judge input. Neither target values nor retrospective error reports were sent to running workers.

A fresh-checkout check also found that TypeScript test imports widened the inferred build root, emitting the CLI under a different path from its package entry points. Old build files could conceal that problem. Source emission and no-emit test typechecking are now verified separately by the clean-build repair; the recorded quoting runs used TypeScript source, not those compiled CLI files.

They are unvalidated guidance changes, not evidence of a behavioral improvement. The next evaluation requires repaired chronology and newly frozen inputs; no criterion was relaxed, no confirmation result was established, and no production quote was authorized.
