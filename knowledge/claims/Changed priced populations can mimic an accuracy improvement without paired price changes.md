Aggregate error changes across different priced populations do not establish improved prices on matched cases.
The matched-first comparison in [[Keller retained offline backend learning evidence]] preserves three numeric v3 cases and five numeric v4 cases, with identical prices and targets in all three common pairs.
Every paired APE change was zero; the apparent full-cohort MAPE movement from 55.47% to 52.70% reflected different coverage rather than a paired numerical improvement.
The common three-case MAPE remained 55.47%, and signed-error population variance remained 3,789.30 squared percentage points with denominator n.
The [[Keller Git-pinned experimental reports 68c8201]] distinguishes that population variance from sample variances in the separate historical register comparisons.
Missing, denied, held and preflight-blocked cases were not converted into numeric zero errors or removed from their declared selection population.
