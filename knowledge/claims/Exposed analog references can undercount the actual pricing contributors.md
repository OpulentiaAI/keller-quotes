Displayed or exposed analog references can undercount the evidence that actually contributes to a price.
The independently reconstructed 50-case replay in [[Keller Git-pinned experimental reports 68c8201]] used 372 contributors while exposing 234 references; 230 contributors were unscreened and seven top-three candidates were quarantined.
The original source-exclusion and cutoff checks passed only their exposed-reference boundary, not a complete contributor audit.
The guarded replay moved from 48 priced, two held and eight all-pass to 47 priced, three held and nine all-pass on the same 50 cases.
That safety-boundary change had mixed numerical outcomes and did not establish a general accuracy improvement.
