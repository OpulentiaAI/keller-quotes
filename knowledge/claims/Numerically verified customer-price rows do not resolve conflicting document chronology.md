Numeric customer-price reconciliation does not independently prove that the price-bearing PDF version existed before an RFQ cutoff.
The builder audit in [[Keller retained chronology and decision-state learning evidence]] shows that the original implementation checked Inquiry Date against metadata while using the By footer only as a table terminator.
A later printed version therefore could coexist with an earlier accepted metadata date.
The full-union audit examined 7,733 distinct document identities across six scopes, found 411 with a metadata conflict and 38 whose By date was on or after at least one applicable cutoff.
These are distinct-document counts over the audited union, not additional worker attempts or a measure of quote accuracy.
Clear pre-cutoff dates were consistent with metadata but still did not prove first issuance; conflicting version evidence and unavailable issuance proof must not be collapsed into the same assertion.
The [[Keller Git-pinned experimental reports 68c8201]] preserves the original chronology blocker and the unchanged original grades rather than silently substituting targets.
