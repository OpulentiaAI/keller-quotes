The 107 recorded customer amounts are verified source observations, of which 105 are comparable to the associated frozen case.
The [[Keller original-source audit 2026-10-04]] preserves five inquiry/footer-date conflicts and separates 139 cases without a selected document group at the target quantity from four with a group but no target-quantity amount.
These conditions explain support limits without treating an unsupported document as a false internal-calculation target.

The [[Keller historical evidence contract cf1ee17]] requires a separate primary send event binding the original PDF/letter, identity, full amount and RFQ before issued-quote identity is established.
Customer acceptance needs its own independently sourced accepted-order event, not the PDF, a PO-looking string, posted won history or shipment existence.
Inquiry/header metadata, archive mtime and a consistent footer do not independently prove when the exact PDF version was available.
Historical targets therefore remain internal-calculation targets, not automatically customer-accepted prices or actual costs.
