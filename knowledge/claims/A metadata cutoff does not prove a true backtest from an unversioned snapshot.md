An exclusive quote-time metadata cutoff does not prove a true backtest from an unversioned extract.
The source and evaluation contracts in [[Keller Git-pinned experimental reports 68c8201]] exclude known future quote, letter, revision and outcome events while retaining the possibility of later unversioned edits inside earlier-looking rows.
Freezing those bytes makes a replay reproducible but does not reconstruct what a quote-time worker actually knew.
The printed-version conflicts in [[Keller retained chronology and decision-state learning evidence]] demonstrate a separate concrete date failure that numeric verification did not catch.
A snapshot replay, a historically authorized reissue and a preselected prospective confirmation population therefore remain different evidence, even when arithmetic and metadata checks pass.
