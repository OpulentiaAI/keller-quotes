The reconciliation in [[Keller retained physical source and integrity audits]] finds that exported calculation breaks can differ from letter-price fields even when quantity and quote linkage are comparable.
The source contract in [[Keller Git-pinned experimental reports 68c8201]] therefore requires explicit register or corpus selection rather than silently substituting one basis for another.
Customer-PDF outcomes remain unknown, and legacy posted-history won/open labels are not independently verified wins, losses, payment or revenue.
The [[Keller historical evidence contract cf1ee17]] keeps these prices separate from current manufacturing costs, complete actual-cost ground truth and first-issuance evidence.
