Historical-source reissue fidelity and independent price prediction measure different tasks.
The reissue ledger in [[Keller retained reissue learning evidence]] retains 24 attempted workflows, including 12 invalid or stopped v1 fixtures and partly repeated corrected batches.
The two final batches contained six attempts: the original result was 5/6 all-pass, while the separately retained adjudicated result was 6/6 on unchanged submissions.
The original judgments, grading failures and calibration disagreement remain part of that history.
The [[Keller Git-pinned experimental reports 68c8201]] keeps the separate screened prediction diagnostic at 9/50 all-pass with 47 priced and three holds.
Pooling those populations or presenting reissue success as general live quoting accuracy would misstate the measured task and denominator.
