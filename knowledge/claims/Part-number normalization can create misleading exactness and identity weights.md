Normalized part identifiers can receive strong retrieval weight without establishing a shared manufacturing identity.
The replay in [[Keller retained numerical mechanism experiments]] shows uncertainty markers stripped from a partially masked identifier, producing a prefix-plus-same-customer score rather than a demonstrated exact component match.
The worker's later transfer from another component magnified the miss without a justified scale adjustment.
The separate namespace assessment in [[Keller paired historical replay report 2026-10-04]] likewise distinguishes customer/part identity from cross-customer descriptions and unsupported identifier-only matching.
Missing customer identity is provisional rather than proof of a conflict, and an exact textual identity still does not prove revision, geometry or process equivalence.
The [[Keller Git-pinned experimental reports 68c8201]] retains these mechanisms without endorsing a hard global customer filter.
