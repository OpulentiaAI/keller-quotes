Tool calls, numeric proposals and attempted quote cases have different measurement grains.
The original trajectory account in [[Keller Git-pinned experimental reports 68c8201]] retains 406 delivered MCP calls across 18 attempted workflows with no all-pass; its numerical account separately retains 27 actual quote-tool calls, including denied and unpriced responses.
Repeated final-answer copies are not new calls, and repeated identical proposals are not independent successes.
Each retained client-pilot plan in [[Keller retained client-pilot diagnostic candidates]] declares at most 250 cases, concurrency one, a 120-second command limit, a 300-second total limit and two attempts.
Those are frozen execution budgets, not measured cold-start latency or proof that every prerequisite completed within the budget.
Failed runtime attempts remain recorded rather than being hidden by later readiness probes or counted as accurate holds.
