A matched comparison that changes registers measures a source-basis difference, not a code-only improvement.
The retained two-register experiment in [[Keller Git-pinned experimental reports 68c8201]] used the same 50 customer-PDF target cases and priced 48 in both conditions.
The internal calculation register produced 9/50 all-pass and 75.5361% priced-case median APE; the verified register produced 8/50 and 63.3716%.
A lower median therefore coexisted with worse all-pass, without establishing a blanket accuracy gain.
The fixed 250-case internal-target diagnostic was a different population and could not serve as the denominator for that comparison.
Source-exclusion and cutoff observations were the old exposed-reference checks, with later contributor and chronology limitations preserved rather than upgraded into prospective proof.
