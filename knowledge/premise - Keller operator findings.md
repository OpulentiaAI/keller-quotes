# Intent

Retain Keller's source-grounded operational, historical-evaluation and evidence-quality findings as a typed operator knowledge graph.
Claims distinguish directly checked bytes, dated measurements, source-fitness limitations and unresolved obligations, with explicit population, grain, code version and supporting source records.
This graph documents the investigations through 2026-10-04 and the source-distribution follow-up; it is published on 2026-10-07 without refreshing the private business sources or re-running historical experiments.
The [[map - Keller operator findings]] provides the entry point, and the source notes retain hashes and references rather than reproducing private records.

# Out-of-scope

- Hidden case targets, customer/part-specific diagnoses, raw prices, private RFQs, ledgers, PDFs, transcripts and database exports.
- Credentials, connection strings, personal-device information and private absolute paths.
- Active price prescriptions, automatic knowledge-store activation, customer release, production writes and scheduler activation.
- Treating candidate joins, posted won labels, shipments, cost-field names, agent assertions or local receipts as independent customer acceptance or complete actual cost.
- Treating unknown evidence as a lost order, a verified negative, a false target or a quoting-accuracy score.
- Feeding these retrospective outcomes, tags, future costs or archived measurements into blinded workers or judges.
- Claiming current costs, improved pricing, an untouched confirmation set, live completion accuracy or business outperformance without the distinct evidence required for that claim.
