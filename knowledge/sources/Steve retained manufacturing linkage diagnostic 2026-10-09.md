## Scope and results

This follows [[Steve frozen development evidence diagnostic 2026-10-09]] with a
**separate operator-only metadata audit**, not a new evaluation arm. It addresses
whether the absence of same-part candidates in the frozen customer-PDF register
also holds for the larger retained internal register. It does not substitute
internal calculations for issued prices or authorize any original document for
the frozen pricing workers or WonderSearch.

The internal register contains 179,608 quantity-break rows and 40,111 quote/item
groups. Only the 208 already development-tagged requests were matched; the other
42 cases were not drilled into. All **250 cohort source quote numbers** were
excluded from candidate linkage before original component inspection. No target
price, vendor-price, cost, rate or markup field was extracted or compared.

| Metadata observation | Development cases / 208 |
|---|---:|
| Same normalized part, including the excluded source | 208 |
| Same normalized part outside all cohort source quotes | 7 |
| Also the same customer | 3 |
| Also passes the recorded quote/revision/letter date cutoff | 3 |

The final three cases link to four distinct prior quote/item groups; each of the
four candidate quotes has one item in the internal register. Four of the seven
cases with external same-part matches have no same-customer match. Part-number
equality alone is therefore not a safe ownership/identity join. The strict
recorded-date check applies to every quantity-break row, not just a convenient
representative row. Blank optional revision timestamps remain unknown, not
independent proof of source availability before cutoff.

## Original component observations

The three selected original DBFs matched their retained manifest hashes before
reading. Only the four non-cohort candidate quote IDs were admitted to component
decoding; unmatched/deleted records were not used as manufacturing facts.

| Original table | Active linked rows | Development cases with rows / 208 | Observation |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| QUOTEM | 3 | 2 | Positive stock length/width, thickness and weight fields; nonblank material IDs. Part length/width fields are zero. |
| QUOTOPER | 36 | 3 | Nonblank operation/setup/run formula IDs and positive SU_V1/RUN_V1 inputs. Direct SUTIME/RUNTIME fields are zero. |
| QUOTCAD | 0 | 0 | No linked CAD record in this particular retained table. |

There were zero deleted rows for these four quote IDs in the inspected tables;
this is not a claim about deletion rates in the entire snapshot. Record counts
were independently cross-checked using the retained schema inventory.

**A zero direct-time field is not proof that no estimating inputs exist.** The
operation records carry formula references and variables through a different
representation. Those variables are not automatically minutes, actual time or
current labor cost. [[Resolved legacy formula references do not validate manufacturing cost aggregation]]
continues to apply. No formulas were executed and no inferred labor charges were
generated by this audit.

## Limits and next handoff

Candidate revision fields are nonblank, but the requests have no independently
resolved revision to compare. Drawing references and material names are blank in
the candidate register; material IDs and geometric fields in QUOTEM need their
catalog meaning, units and applicability checked separately. Row presence does
not prove complete routing, procurement UOM, yield, outside processing or a
supplier-backed current cost. A 2026 snapshot plus earlier recorded dates does
not establish what a historical estimator could have read at that cutoff.

This exact-identity route provides source leads for **3/208**, not a basis for
claiming archive sufficiency or absence for the other 205. Different-part process
analogs, drawing content and other source families were not assessed here. The
next source handoff must resolve engineering identity/revision and applicable
formula semantics under an authorized original-document scope, then independently
support current procurement/routing inputs. Do not add these records to the
frozen run or report 3/208 as a price improvement.

The retained historical result is still **45/250**. Retrieval quality, current-cost
accuracy and realized-margin accuracy remain unmeasured. No production, provider,
pricing-policy or human-review gate changed.

## Reproduce privately

`evals/diagnose-internal-linkage.py` pins the cases, internal register, prior
development diagnostic and all three original DBF hashes. Its summary is
`evals/steve-internal-linkage-diagnostic.public.json`; case and quote identifiers
remain in owner-private outputs outside Git. Run with the original source
directory, the retained development diagnostic and a fresh private output path:

```sh
env -i PATH="$PATH" HOME="$HOME" LANG=C.UTF-8 python3 -B \
  evals/diagnose-internal-linkage.py \
  "$CASES" "$INTERNAL_REGISTER" "$DEVELOPMENT_DIAGNOSTIC" \
  "$ORIGINAL_DBF_DIRECTORY" "$PRIVATE_OUTPUT"
```

Eight synthetic tests cover original-byte pinning, truncation, excluded/deleted
row handling, stable missing-row output, strict calendar/cutoff checks, formula
variables versus direct times and the public report's script fingerprint. Private
verification also checks byte-identical replay, refusal to overwrite, wrong input
hashes, nonprivate output, missing originals and optimized Python. All prior
private diagnostic versions are preserved rather than relabelled.
